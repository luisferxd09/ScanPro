## 🌸 What it can do

- 📷 **Google AI scanner**: better focus and edge detection, offline
- 🌍 **15 languages** with options for each country (dates, currency, paper size, ID names)
- 📄 **PDF tools**: split, compress, password, watermark, page numbers
- ✍️ **Sign**, write, highlight, strike out and stamp
- 🔤 **Text recognition (OCR)**, translate, read aloud and dictate
- 🧠 **Ask your documents**, contract review and form filling
- 🗂️ **Life folders** with expiry reminders, expenses and inventory
- 🎓 Summaries and study cards, PowerPoint and Word
- 📥 Opens PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, EPUB, ZIP, HEIC, TIFF and more
- 👋 Welcome screen, step-by-step guides, sounds and haptic feedback

## ⬇️ Installation

1. Go to **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and download the **APK**.
2. Open it on your phone.
3. If Android asks, allow **“Install unknown apps”**.
4. Done! Open **ScanPro** and choose your language 👋

## 🔒 Privacy

- 📴 **Works offline**
- 🏠 Your photos and documents **never leave your phone**
- 🙅 **No accounts or analytics**; sometimes a short ad when exporting
- 📷 The camera only turns on when you tap **“Take photo”**
